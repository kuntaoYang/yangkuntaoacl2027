## Likely causes
- **Proven observation:** sequence parsing and state tracking were error-prone throughout (`021`, steps **19–30**, **37–42**, **96–105`).
- **Hypothesis:** the model lacked a robust skill/workflow for biological sequence diffing and Golden Gate junction derivation, leading to ad hoc reasoning and overhang invention.
- **Not infrastructure failure:** tools and package install worked (`021`, steps **11–16**). This was not blocked by the environment.
- **Model capability limit:** sustained symbolic accuracy across long biosequence manipulations appears weak here, especially maintaining consistent boundaries and constraints over many steps.

## Generic harness fixes
- Add a **structured biosequence skill** for FASTA parsing, reverse-complement, boundary diffing, and junction extraction.
- Require **artifact validation gates** before final answer: FASTA format check, header-spec check, annealing-length/Tm check, and consistency checks between claimed junctions and derived boundaries.
- Encourage a **plan-then-verify workflow** with explicit intermediate tables rather than freeform exploratory scripting.
