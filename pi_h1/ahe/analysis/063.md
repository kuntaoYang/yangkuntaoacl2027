- Require **machine-checkable assertions** to gate finalization; if any required check fails, prevent completion.  
- Provide a **structured skill/tool** for sequence extraction, translation parsing, linker insertion, and GC-window optimization instead of free-form bash/python.  
- Provide **API wrappers/examples** for fpbase queries to reduce endpoint/JSON mistakes.
